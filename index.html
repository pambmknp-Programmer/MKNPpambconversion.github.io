Conversion Information

1 Board Foot (BF) =

0.00236 Cubic Meters (m³)

1 Cubic Meter (m³) =

423.776 Board Feet (BF)

Form Factor (Felled):

Typical: 0.9 (accounts for log shape)

Form Factor (Standing):

Typical: 0.4 (accounts for taper)

Note: A board foot is a unit of volume used in lumber measurement. It equals the volume of a board 1 foot long, 1 foot wide, and 1 inch thick.
